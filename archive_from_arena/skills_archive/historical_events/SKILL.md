# Historical Knowledge Reference

## Wars and Conflicts
- **World War I**: 1914-1918 (US entry: Apr 1917)
- **World War II**: 1939-1945 (US entry: Dec 1941; Europe theater ends May 1945, Pacific Aug 1945)
- **Korean War**: 1950-1953
- **Vietnam War**: 1955-1975 (major US involvement 1965-1973)
- **Gulf War (Desert Storm)**: Aug 1990 - Feb 1991

## Economic Events
- **Great Depression**: 1929-1939 (stock crash Oct 29, 1929)
- **Black Monday**: Oct 19, 1987 (DJIA fell 22.6%)
- **Nixon Shock / end of gold standard**: Aug 15, 1971
- **Bretton Woods Agreement**: Jul 1944 (fixed exchange rates, gold at $35/oz)
- **Plaza Accord**: Sep 22, 1985 (USD devaluation agreement)
- **Dot-com bubble burst**: Mar 2000 - Oct 2002
- **Great Recession**: Dec 2007 - Jun 2009 (Lehman collapse Sep 15, 2008)

## Fiscal/Monetary Milestones
- **Federal Reserve Act**: Dec 23, 1913
- **Social Security Act**: Aug 14, 1935
- **US fiscal year change**: pre-1976 = Jul 1 - Jun 30; from FY1977 = Oct 1 - Sep 30; **transition quarter** = Jul-Sep 1976 (TQ)
- **Treasury Bulletin**: published monthly through 1982, quarterly from 1983

## Country Groups
- **Gold Bloc (1933-1936)**: France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Italy, Poland, Switzerland
- **NATO founding (1949)**: US, UK, France, Canada, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Italy, Portugal
- **Marshall Plan recipients (1948-1952)**: UK, France, Italy, W. Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Austria, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Switzerland, Greece, Turkey, Ireland, Iceland, Portugal
- **G7**: US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Canada, Japan (formalized 1975)
- **G8**: G7 + Russia (1997-2014)

## Treasury/Government Name Changes
- **War Department** -> **Department of Defense** (National Security Act, 1947)
- **"National defense"** consolidated War Dept + Navy Dept spending from 1947
- **Bureau of Internal Revenue** -> **Internal Revenue Service** (1952)
- **Bureau of the Budget** -> **Office of Management and Budget** (1970)
- **Post Office Department** -> **US Postal Service** (1971, removed from cabinet)

## Date Arithmetic Patterns
When a question says "N years before/after [event]", resolve the event to its start year:
- "1 year before WW2" = 1939 - 1 = **1938**
- "2 years after Korean War" = 1953 + 2 = **1955** (uses end year for "after")
- "during the Great Depression" = fiscal years **1930-1939**
- "pre-war" typically means before WW2 (pre-1939) unless context indicates WW1
