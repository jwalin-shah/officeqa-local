# Fiscal Year Rules for Treasury Bulletins

## Critical: Fiscal Year != Calendar Year

The U.S. federal fiscal year (FY) does NOT align with the calendar year.
Getting this wrong is the #1 source of errors.

## Pre-1977 Fiscal Year
- FY runs July 1 (Year-1) to June 30 (Year)
- Example: **FY 1940** = July 1, 1939 through June 30, 1940
- This applies to ALL bulletins published before October 1976

## Post-1976 Fiscal Year (Transition)
- Transition Quarter (TQ): July 1, 1976 through September 30, 1976
- Starting FY 1977: October 1 (Year-1) to September 30 (Year)
- Example: **FY 1980** = October 1, 1979 through September 30, 1980

## Calendar Year
- Always January 1 through December 31
- Tables sometimes show "Calendar yr." or "Cal. yr." row
- If no calendar year row exists, sum the 12 monthly rows (Jan-Dec)

## How to Read Table Rows

| Row label | What it means |
|-----------|---------------|
| `1940` (annual row, no month) | Usually FISCAL year 1940 total |
| `Calendar yr. 1940` or `Cal. yr.` | Calendar year 1940 total (Jan-Dec) |
| `January` or `1940-January` | Individual month of January |
| `1st quarter` | First 3 months of fiscal year |
| `Transition quarter` or `TQ` | Jul-Sep 1976 only |

## Common Traps

1. **"Calendar year 1940 expenditures"** — Do NOT use the row labeled "1940" if it's a fiscal year table. Sum Jan-Dec monthly rows instead, or find a "Calendar yr." row.

2. **"FY 1940 expenditures"** — DO use the row labeled "1940" directly. This IS the fiscal year total.

3. **Monthly sums** — When the question says "sum of individual calendar months," you MUST add up 12 separate monthly rows. The annual row is NOT the same as summing months (due to rounding, adjustments, etc.).

4. **Bulletin vintage** — The same data year may appear in multiple bulletins. Prefer the bulletin published closest to (but after) the data year. Later bulletins may have revised figures.

5. **Pre-1977 monthly order** — In fiscal-year-ordered tables, months run July-June (not Jan-Dec). For calendar year sums, you still want Jan-Dec.
